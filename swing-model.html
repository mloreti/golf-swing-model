At impact

Kinematic model: the hands travel on a circle around a swing centre in the upper chest, which moves with the pressure shift, tilt, posture and height sliders. Hip and chest turn are drawn for sequencing; the club's path and strike come from those body translations plus the plane, direction, release and face sliders. The default swing is calibrated to a centred, square, tour-like strike.

Auto tracking

Finds your body in every frame with MediaPipe Pose, running in this browser. The model (about 6 MB) downloads once from a CDN; the video itself stays on this device. Works best with a full-body, face-on clip. The club is not tracked.

Track points

At each checkpoint, pick a body point and click it on the video. The next point is selected automatically.
